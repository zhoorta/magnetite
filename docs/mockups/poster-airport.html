Poster: Airport

The FAA airport diagram as a meter, with the chart as it is (its names covered, its data blocks replaced by the track). A plane rolls down a runway as the track plays, a different runway for each track (press next track to see it change); the two long parallel runways are also the left and right level and the short one the peak; the 30 bars east of the right runway are the bands; the terminal flashes on bass onsets.
