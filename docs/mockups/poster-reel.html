Poster: Reel

A colour strip down the left, black beside it, and the cream tape reel across both (laid out like a second tape-box poster: four windows, no spoke). The reel is cropped by the bottom right corner; the track's lettering sits top right on the black. It turns with the level, its windows swell with left, right and the mean, and a ring of tape on the hub grows and shrinks with how far into the track you are.
