Poster: Circuit

A textbook cover: album in small capitals, title and artist in rounded lowercase, flush right. The schematic below is the meter. The seven straps are seven bands and fill cell by cell (the pale tick holds the peak); an amplifier lights when any band reaches its row, the gate behind it when two do; the two wide gates fill with the left and right level; the domes flash on onsets and the four on the right count the strobe; the boxes hold time played, length and format.
