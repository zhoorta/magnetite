Poster: Pylon

A black-and-white information poster as a meter. The 44 rings behind the title are the spectrum, clearly visible but calm (steady lines, a small slow swell with each band, one colour, a barely visible ripple on each bass onset); the pylon's three tiers are highs, mids and bass; the three bolts are left, loudness and right; the dots are the last three seconds; the three stars are polar spectrum, peaks and onsets.
