Poster: Glow

Glowing line on black. The big disc is the waveform: its upright lines bend with it, deeper when louder. The dots are four bands in four steps. The striped ball turns with the level and leans with the balance; the globe under it closes to a line when the sound is mono. The two rays are RMS and peak, the tall tick on the ruler is the time played. Album and format sit between the top rules, the title is the wordmark.
