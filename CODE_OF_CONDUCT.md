# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep to the subject. This applies to issues, pull requests and any other space of this project.

- Criticise the work, not the person. Assume good faith, and say what you need.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's report tools or a private security advisory on this repository.
